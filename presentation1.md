## Intro
---

The base world will be a sort of Cold War between two godlike beings. Both have their own views of the world and believe that they are the one that knows what is best. We'll call them the Good God and Evil God even though they honestly are both morally grey but with a slight leaning to good vs evil.

---

## Story Leading to Gameplay
---
The Evil God has many people who work under him and due to some of their curiosities, they now have research facilities across different parts of the work. Many are abandoned and all are hidden underground and can only be accessed from one entrance.
The good side would be sent to these facilities for different reasons, collecting intel from PCs, a black box, physical files, and saving civilians.
At the same time, the evil side will send out one of their best operatives in order to intercept the others or will be waiting at the location for them.
